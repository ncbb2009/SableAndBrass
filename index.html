Meet the Designer

Yohanna Mueller

With a passion for creating spaces that feel both luxurious and livable, I founded Sable & Brass Interiors to bring the warmth of coastal living into every home I touch.

My approach blends natural textures, thoughtful color palettes, and curated details to craft interiors that tell your story. Every project begins with listening — understanding how you live, what inspires you, and what home truly means to you.

Our Philosophy

Design With Soul

We believe that great design isn't just about aesthetics — it's about how a space makes you feel the moment you walk in.

Every texture, every tone, every carefully chosen piece serves a purpose: to create an environment where you feel grounded, inspired, and truly at home.

Sable & Brass Interiors draws from the organic beauty of coastal landscapes — the play of light on water, the warmth of sun-bleached wood, the quiet elegance of natural stone — and translates that feeling into interiors that are both refined and effortlessly inviting.
