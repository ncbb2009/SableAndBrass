The Consulting Process

Your Consulting Journey

Industry best practice from first contact to final deliverable — designed so that by the time you leave your session, the path forward is unmistakably clear.

01

Inquiry & Screening

Submit your request through our client intake form. Within 24 hours, we review your project scope and confirm that consulting is the right engagement for your needs and goals.

02

Pre-Session Brief

You receive a detailed pre-session questionnaire. Yohanna studies your responses, your space, photography, and stated goals — arriving prepared with research done, not improvising in real time.

03

Confirmation & Prep

We confirm session type, format (on-site or virtual), date, and logistics. You receive a pre-session guide with recommended materials to have on hand for maximum productivity.

04

The Session

An intensive, focused engagement with Yohanna. Actionable direction, specific vendor and material recommendations, and a clear prioritized roadmap — delivered with characteristic directness.

05

Written Summary Report

Within 48 hours, you receive a comprehensive written summary of every recommendation, vendor contacts, priority sequence, and next steps — a document you can act upon immediately and decisively.

06

Optional Continuity

Many clients retain Yohanna as their ongoing design advisor — available for follow-up sessions at any stage. Continuity clients receive priority scheduling and a dedicated communication line.
