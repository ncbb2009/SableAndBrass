The Design Process

Your Complete Design Journey

An eight-step partnership built on the industry's highest standards — refined over fifteen years and hundreds of projects into a process that makes the extraordinary feel inevitable.

01

Discovery Call

A personal conversation with Yohanna to explore your vision, lifestyle, aesthetic instincts, and what your ideal home feels like to live in. This is not an interview — it is a creative collaboration.

02

Site Assessment

Yohanna visits your property for an immersive walkthrough — measuring, photographing, studying light conditions, architectural features, and listening to the story the space is trying to tell.

03

Concept Direction

We present a curated concept board, color palette, material direction, and spatial vision. This is where creative direction is set and refined collaboratively — the foundation for everything that follows.

04

Design Development

Full room plans, furniture selections, custom millwork specifications, lighting design, material samples, and a detailed investment summary. Every decision documented and presented with complete transparency.

05

Client Approval

You review, revise, and approve the complete design. Nothing moves forward without your full confidence. We don't proceed until the plan reflects your vision — perfected and exactly right.

06

Procurement

All purchasing managed through our trade accounts. Custom pieces commissioned. Imported items coordinated. Lead times tracked with absolute precision so every element arrives when it needs to.

07

Project Management

Where construction or renovation is involved, Yohanna works in direct collaboration with your contractors — reviewing work, approving materials on-site, ensuring every trade adheres precisely to the design intent.

08

The Reveal

You see your completed home for the first time. The gasp. The quiet. The overwhelming sense of rightness. This moment — this specific, unrepeatable experience — is what Yohanna designs toward in every single project.
