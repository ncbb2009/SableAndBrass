The Staging Process

Your Staging Journey

A meticulous, proven six-step process that transforms your property from a house into a destination — and your listing from overlooked to oversubscribed.

01

Property Assessment

Yohanna conducts an on-site walkthrough with your listing agent. She assesses architectural strengths, identifies staging opportunities, and defines your target buyer's lifestyle profile.

02

Staging Proposal

Within 48 hours you receive a detailed staging proposal: scope of work, investment, timeline, and before/after concept boards for key rooms. Nothing ambiguous, nothing open-ended.

03

Curation & Sourcing

We curate furniture, art, soft goods, and accessories from our premium inventory and exclusive vendor relationships — tailored precisely to the property's target buyer and listing price point.

04

Installation Day

Our professional team arrives on schedule and completes the full installation within a single day. Yohanna oversees every placement personally — nothing is positioned without her eye confirming it.

05

Photography & Launch

Your staged property is photography-ready. Our compositions are built for the camera — every angle composed, every vignette balanced for maximum impact in listing photos and buyer walkthroughs.

06

De-Stage on Closing

Upon closing, our team removes all staging inventory within the agreed timeframe — coordinating seamlessly with your escrow timeline. Zero disruption to the final transaction.
